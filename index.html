Our story

Two roasters, one very small drum, and a stubborn idea.

9's Cafe started in a garage in 2016 with a second-hand 5-kilo roaster and a simple belief: coffee should be bought from people you could call by name.

Nine years later we still buy directly from every farm we work with, still pay above fair-trade rates, and still roast in batches small enough to taste every single one.
